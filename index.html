Dr Ram Prasad Krishnamoorthy


My expertise spans classical machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing, agentic AI, generative AI, and LLM/SLM fine-tuning, alongside biometrics, software development, and algorithm design. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science with the distinction "Sobresaliente Cum Laude" from Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, Spain. I was honored with the prestigious European Union Marie Curie Fellowship twice — for my doctoral research at Universidad Autónoma de Madrid and my postdoctoral research at Dublin City University, Ireland — and my Ph.D. work was nominated for the prestigious European Biometrics Research and Industry Award. Throughout my career I have collaborated with industry and academic laboratories across Europe and India, contributing to technology transfer, research, and consultancy.

I also held visiting researcher positions at the University of Twente, Netherlands, and Halmstad University, Sweden, and graduated with a degree in Computer Science from Chennai Mathematical Institute, India.

Recent Projects

  • Prokraya-AI — Large-scale procurement software powered by agents and agentic AI; designed a layered architecture spanning knowledge, prompts, skills, feedback, memory, and workflow, integrating both frontier models (OpenAI, Claude) and self-hosted open-weight models (Gemma, Llama, Qwen), with per-model token usage monitoring.
  • Spend Categorization — Fine-tuned Small Language Models (Qwen; Mistral-7B via Snowflake Cortex AI) to predict Segment, Family, and Class levels of the UNSPSC hierarchy from purchase line-item descriptions.
  • ITSM-Assist — Fine-tuned a Qwen SLM for intent classification of support tickets, routing queries to RAG-based agents; replaced a proprietary frontier model and added intent summaries with confidence scores.
  • Multi-SLM Grading System — Fine-tuned an ensemble of SLMs (Qwen, Llama, Gemma) to automate rubric-based exam grading with normalized scores, qualitative feedback, and automated visual performance summaries.
  • Bot Traffic Detection — Two-stage semi-supervised pipeline combining unsupervised anomaly detection with a boosting-based classifier to detect human-mimicking bots in Google Analytics 4 web traffic.
  • Electric Meter OCR — Trained a YOLOv11 oriented-bounding-box model to detect and deskew electric meter components in uncontrolled mobile-camera images for downstream OCR of readings and serial numbers.
  • MRN Receipt OCR with Handwriting Recognition — YOLOv11-based layout detection and perspective correction combined with multi-modal OCR models (e.g. GLM) and rule-guided prompting to extract mixed printed/handwritten receipt data.
